# Concept art and editorial illustration prompts

Describe visual properties directly rather than naming living artists.

## 1. Editorial technology metaphor

```text
Create an editorial illustration about {technology topic} using the metaphor of {physical scene}.
One clear focal idea, limited {three-color palette}, bold geometric shapes, subtle paper texture,
and thoughtful negative space. The image should communicate tension between {two concepts}
without literal labels. 3:2 landscape. No text, corporate logos, or imitation of a named artist.
```

## 2. Near-future workspace

```text
Design a believable near-future workspace for {profession} in {location}. Technology should feel
like an incremental evolution of current tools: practical interfaces, ergonomic hardware,
repairable materials, and visible human use. Wide establishing shot, natural morning light,
grounded industrial design, detailed but uncluttered. Avoid hologram clichés, impossible screens,
brand marks, and sterile empty rooms.
```

## 3. World-building location sheet

```text
Create a location design sheet for {place} in a world shaped by {environmental/social condition}.
Include one wide establishing view and three small detail studies of architecture, material,
transport, and everyday objects. Maintain consistent construction logic, climate response, scale,
and color palette. Neutral presentation background, no labels or fictional writing.
```

## 4. Character profession study

```text
Design an original character whose profession is {role} in {setting}. Communicate the occupation
through practical clothing, tools, posture, and wear patterns rather than stereotypes. Full-body
front three-quarter view plus two close details. Natural proportions, culturally researched
materials, neutral background. No existing franchise elements, logos, or ornamental clutter.
```

## 5. Isometric process illustration

```text
Create an isometric illustration explaining {process} in four connected stages. Use consistent
scale, a clean grid, simplified objects, one accent color per stage, and visible directional flow
created by composition rather than arrows or text. Light neutral background, crisp vector-like
edges, 16:9. Avoid unreadable labels and unnecessary decorative elements.
```

## 6. Climate data cover image

```text
Create a report cover visual about {climate subject} by combining a recognizable {landscape/object}
with an abstract representation of {data pattern}. Serious editorial tone, restrained blue-earth
palette with one warning accent, atmospheric depth, and a clear central hierarchy. A4 portrait with
the upper third quiet for title layout. No generated text, catastrophe spectacle, or false charts.
```

## 7. Architectural mood frame

```text
Visualize {building type} in {geographic context} at {time/weather}. Emphasize how the architecture
responds to climate, circulation, and local materials. Human figures provide scale without becoming
the subject. Eye-level 24 mm lens look, physically plausible light, detailed landscape integration,
3:2. Avoid impossible cantilevers, duplicate people, illegible signage, or generic luxury styling.
```

## 8. Abstract music artwork

```text
Create abstract cover artwork for music described as {tempo, instruments, emotional arc}. Translate
rhythm into {shape language}, timbre into {texture}, and dynamics into spatial density. Use a limited
{palette}, one dominant focal movement, tactile print-like detail, and a square crop. No typography,
faces, instruments, logos, or imitation of an existing album cover.
```

## Common fixes

- Reduce the concept to one visual metaphor before adding style details.
- Specify construction and material logic for believable environments.
- Request a neutral sheet background for design turnarounds and studies.
- Exclude text whenever final copy will be added in layout software.

## Put the prompts into practice

These briefs describe visual properties instead of imitating a named living artist. They work with
any compatible image model; for browser-based generation and reference-image refinement, you can
adapt them in [Rao Edits](https://raoedits.top/). Disclosure: Rao Edits maintains this open
