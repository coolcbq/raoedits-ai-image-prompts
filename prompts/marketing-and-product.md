# Marketing and product photography prompts

Replace every value in `{braces}`. Keep the product identity and geometry explicit when
using an input image.

## 1. Clean ecommerce hero

```text
Create a premium ecommerce hero image for {product} aimed at {audience}.
Place one product at a three-quarter angle on a seamless {background color} studio sweep.
Use a large softbox from upper left, a subtle rim light, realistic contact shadow, and
controlled reflections that reveal the material without hiding the label. Leave 35% clean
negative space on the {left/right} for a headline. Photorealistic commercial photography,
85 mm lens look, crisp edges, accurate product proportions, 4:5 portrait crop.
Avoid extra products, floating objects, invented text, distorted packaging, and hard glare.
```

## 2. Feature exploded view

```text
Create a technically plausible exploded-view visualization of {product} showing
{three to five components}. Arrange the parts along a single assembly axis with consistent
spacing and subtle guide lines. Use a neutral light-gray background, soft orthographic
lighting, matte materials, and one accent color for the key component. Wide 16:9 layout
with space for numbered callouts. Do not invent internal parts or add unreadable labels.
```

## 3. Lifestyle problem-and-solution scene

```text
Show {product} solving {specific customer problem} in a believable {location}.
The product is the visual focus but the user’s action explains the benefit. Natural window
light, candid composition, realistic surfaces, restrained color grade, documentary brand
photography. Reserve a clean area at the top for campaign copy. 3:2 landscape.
Avoid staged smiles, excessive props, implausible usage, and visible third-party logos.
```

## 4. Material macro detail

```text
Create a macro product photograph emphasizing the {material/finish} of {product}.
Focus on {specific edge, control, texture, or seam}; use shallow depth of field while
keeping the key functional detail sharp. Grazing side light should reveal micro-texture,
with a dark-to-neutral background and physically believable reflections. 1:1 crop.
Preserve the original geometry and avoid dust, fingerprints, scratches, or invented text.
```

## 5. Seasonal campaign without clichés

```text
Design a {season/event} campaign visual for {product} using {palette} and subtle cues such
as {materials or environmental detail}. Keep the product unchanged and central. Build a
refined still-life composition with layered depth, soft directional light, and an upscale
editorial mood. 4:5 crop with text-safe space. Avoid generic holiday icons, confetti,
over-decoration, and fake promotional text.
```

## 6. Marketplace comparison layout

```text
Create a clean comparison visual containing three consistent views of {product}:
{front}, {side}, and {in-use detail}. Use identical lighting and scale, a white background,
thin neutral dividers, and generous margins. Keep the center area free for later typography.
High-key catalog photography, 16:9. Do not generate labels, badges, claims, or competitor marks.
```

## 7. Transparent-background packshot

```text
Isolate {product} as a polished catalog packshot on a truly transparent background.
Preserve exact silhouette, colors, logo placement, controls, and proportions from the input.
Use soft studio lighting with a subtle grounding shadow delivered as a separate-looking
soft alpha edge. Centered composition, high-resolution intent. No added props, text, crop,
halo, color spill, or reconstructed details.
```

## 8. Product launch key visual

```text
Create a launch key visual for {product} communicating {single benefit} through the scene,
not through text. Place the product emerging from {visual metaphor} with controlled motion
and particles, but keep its form completely readable. Cinematic backlight, one strong accent
color, deep tonal range, premium technology advertising aesthetic. 16:9 and 9:16-safe center.
Avoid explosions, visual clutter, fake UI, unreadable lettering, and altered product design.
```

## Common fixes

- **Wrong geometry:** state which shapes, controls, and proportions must remain unchanged.
- **Unreadable labels:** remove generated text and add typography during layout.
- **Plastic-looking materials:** specify roughness, reflection size, and edge behavior.
- **Weak hierarchy:** ask for one hero product and fewer supporting props.
